# Storm Royale

![Storm Royale poster](poster.svg)

A top-down battle royale shooter that runs in your browser. Drop in against bots, survive the closing storm, and be the last one standing. You get 3 lives per match.

## Features
- Modes: Battle Royale, Zero Build, Blitz
- 30 outfits and 7 gliders, a Locker, a daily Item Shop, a free Battle Pass and a weekly skin quest
- Coins and XP earned by playing (saved in your browser)
- Named map locations, a player ID, and touch controls for phones (WASD + mouse on desktop)

## Play
Open `index.html` in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages (Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch) and open your Pages link.
